A make.fun entry for "Last Out of Office: The AI That Went Barista" — one out-of-office auto-reply from an AI agent that quit its job to become a barista, and now silently judges every customer by their coffee order.

## The entry

> Thank you for your email. I am out of office permanently — I quit to become a barista and now silently score every customer by their order: oat-milk flat white, 9/10; 'coffee coffee', decaf, no eye contact.

206 characters, 37 words, 1 line. The barista does not serve coffee, it scores people by their order: the oat-milk flat white earns a near-perfect 9/10, while the customer who orders a "coffee coffee" gets no score at all — just decaf, and no eye contact.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python stdlib only) verifies the entry against the gate in RULES.txt — UTF-8 text, 1 to 280 characters, not only white space, a single line — then writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
